Houseplant care does not need to be complicated

Every houseplant has different needs, from how often it should be watered to how much sunlight it receives. This guide brings the most important care information together in one place so you can spend less time searching and more time helping your plants thrive.

Snake Plant

Description

Snake plants are hardy, low-maintenance houseplants that prefer indirect light but can tolerate lower-light conditions. Allow the soil to dry completely between waterings, typically watering every two to three weeks.

Plant Facts

  • Light: Bright light
  • Water: Every 2–3 weeks
  • Difficulty: Easy

Aloe Plant

Description

Aloe vera prefers bright light and dry soil. Place it near a sunny window and allow the soil to dry completely before watering again. Overwatering is one of the most common problems when caring for aloe.

Plant Facts

  • Light: Bright light
  • Water: Every 2–3 weeks
  • Difficulty: Easy
